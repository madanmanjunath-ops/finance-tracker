Privacy Policy

Prism Finance Tracker · Last updated: 27 July 2026

This policy explains what data Prism Finance Tracker ("the app", "we") collects, why, who it is shared with, and your choices. The app is operated by Prism Connect Solutions ("we"), based in Karnataka, India. Questions: support@prismconnectsolutions.com.

1. What we collect

We do not ask for or store your bank login credentials, card numbers in full, OTPs, or passwords to any third-party service. The app never connects directly to your bank.

2. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database, in a row tied to your user account and protected by Row-Level Security, which technically restricts each row to its owner. A copy is also cached in your browser so the app works offline. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

We use a small number of processors to run the service. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

The optional Gmail import runs as a script inside your own Google account and forwards email text to the app's ingest endpoint. You control it and can stop it at any time.

4. AI processing

When you use an AI feature, only the text needed for that feature (for example, the body of an email you're importing, or a question you type) is sent to Anthropic to generate the result. AI output is generated automatically and may be inaccurate; see the disclaimer in our Terms.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your database row and its contents are deleted. Forwarded email text is processed to extract a transaction and stored as part of your data; it is not kept separately.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, export, or delete your data at any time from within the app, or by contacting support@prismconnectsolutions.com. Depending on where you live (including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the GDPR where applicable), you may have additional rights to withdraw consent or lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority.

7. Security

We use Row-Level Security, encrypted transport, and keep all secret API keys on the server (never in your browser). No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security, and you use the app at your own risk.

8. Children

The app is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; we'll change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app or by email.
